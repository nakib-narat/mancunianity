Printable learning pack

Community Memory and Creative Ageing

A flexible River Irwell resource for observation, discussion and creative response.

Suggested for: adult, older and intergenerational groups

Approximate time: 45-90 minutes

Materials: paper or a notes app, pencil, optional coloured pencils

Learning themes: belonging, memory, place, sound, change, resilience and shared futures

Setting: classroom, community room, home or a safe outdoor viewpoint

Safety and inclusion: Participation and public sharing are different choices. No one is required to disclose personal experience, be recorded or appear in a photograph.

Personal Map

Mark a place associated with work, travel, leisure, change or belonging. Sharing is always optional.

A Sound from the City

Describe or record a sound that represents change or continuity. Agree recording and publication choices separately.

Then, Now, Next

Choose one river feature and discuss what it was, what it is now and what it could become.

Add Your Thread

Create a short reflection about an everyday story that future generations should remember.

Choose a story doorway

Open the Stories from the River collection. Choose one taster, identify the confirmed fact, the visible or historical clue and the question left open. Discuss why a live performance might reveal the story differently from a webpage.

Reflection

What did your group notice that might be missed by someone moving quickly through the city? What should future generations know, protect or change?

Share with care

Groups may send non-personal reflections or resource feedback to mancunianityflows@outlook.com. Do not send children's full names, private contact details or sensitive personal stories.
