River Irwell route information

Walk and Map

Find the permanent map page for the developing Mancunianity Flows route along the River Irwell between Manchester and Salford.

Route and access information in developmentBooking is not yet open. Public walk dates will be announced when confirmed.

The permanent map page

This page will remain at mancunianity.uk/map/ so printed QR codes can continue to point to one stable address.

Provisional area

The developing experience follows connections across the River Irwell between Manchester and Salford. Final start and finish points, distance, duration, surfaces, gradients, crossings, seating, toilets and step-free alternatives will be published only after route testing.

Accessible route information

A structured text-only route, access notes and an accessible map download will appear here when checked. To request information in another format, email nakib.narat@btinternet.com or call 07538 599049.
