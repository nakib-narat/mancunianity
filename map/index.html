Follow the Spider’s Web

A riverside adventure

Trace the River Irwell through a landscape shaped by water, railway engineering, working animals, industry and human connections.

Map available nowThe map is an illustrated interpretation and an appetiser for the developing live experience.

River fact

One river, two cities

The Irwell marks part of the boundary between Manchester and Salford, while bridges, paths and shared histories connect both banks.

Look closely

Bridges through time

Look for layers of movement: people crossing at river level, trains passing above and older railway structures close by.

Follow the thread

A transport web

River, canal, railway, roads, warehouses, workers and horses formed a network that moved goods, ideas and people.

For families

River detective

Can you find one thing living, one thing moving, one thing built and one thing reflected?

About the developing experience

Mancunianity Flows is being shaped with older local Weavers, whose memories, questions and creative choices add lived experience to the public history of the river.

Broad setting: Aviva Studios, River Square and the River Irwell between Manchester and Salford.
