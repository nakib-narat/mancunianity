Privacy notice

How Mancunianity handles website visits, enquiries, participation information and public permissions.

Prepared: 7 September 2026

Who is responsible

Nakib Narat is the current project privacy contact unless responsibility is formally transferred or jointly agreed in writing.

Visiting this website

This static website does not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or non-essential cookies in Version 1. This website does not currently use project-installed analytics, advertising trackers or non-essential cookies. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver, secure and operate the service.

Contacting the project

If you email or telephone, the information you provide will be used to respond, organise appropriate follow-up and keep necessary project records. Please provide only what is needed at first contact.

Applications and participation

Role applications, Weaver participation, volunteering, photography and recording involve separate information and choices. A general enquiry does not give permission to publish a person’s name, story, image or voice.

Retention and rights

Enquiries are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed, in line with the project retention schedule. You may ask what information is held, request correction, object to certain uses, request restriction or deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent where consent is used.

Contact and complaints

Email nakib.narat@btinternet.com or call 07538 599049. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
