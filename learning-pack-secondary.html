Printable learning pack

Secondary Local History and Environment

A flexible River Irwell resource for observation, discussion and creative response.

Suggested for: ages 11-16

Approximate time: one or two lessons

Materials: paper or a notes app, pencil, optional coloured pencils

Learning themes: industrialisation, urbanisation, evidence, labour, flooding, ecology and climate resilience

Setting: classroom, community room, home or a safe outdoor viewpoint

Safety and inclusion: Outdoor use requires school or group risk assessment and responsible adult supervision. Do not ask participants to publish identifiable or sensitive stories.

Fact, Clue or Mystery?

Sort claims into documented fact, visible evidence, interpretation or question still being investigated. Explain what further source would help.

The Transport Web

Map how river, canal, railway, roads, labour, animals, warehouses and international trade depended upon each other.

Boxing Day 2015

Use the sourced flood figures to discuss risk, infrastructure, memory and how cities adapt to extreme rainfall.

Oral History Questions

Draft respectful questions that invite memory without pressuring a person to disclose private experience.

Choose a story doorway

Open the Stories from the River collection. Choose one taster, identify the confirmed fact, the visible or historical clue and the question left open. Discuss why a live performance might reveal the story differently from a webpage.

Reflection

What did your group notice that might be missed by someone moving quickly through the city? What should future generations know, protect or change?

Share with care

Groups may send non-personal reflections or resource feedback to contact@mancunianity.uk. Do not send children's full names, private contact details or sensitive personal stories.
