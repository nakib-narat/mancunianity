Our river, our climate, our future

Environmental learning for the whole family

The river connects industrial history with today's questions about flooding, wildlife, green space, walking, fairness and community action.

Flood detective

Look for flood walls, changes in ground level and places where water might move when the river rises.

Nature connections

Notice plants, birds, river edges and the links between waterways and green spaces.

Future city

Imagine safer, greener routes that connect the Irwell with canals, parks, viaducts and neighbourhoods.

For schools and community groups

Choose a route into the story

Activities can be selected and adapted for different ages, interests and group settings. The aim is not to prescribe one answer, but to encourage observation, evidence, conversation and creative response.

Ages 7-11

River observation, maps, wildlife, transport chains and simple local-history storytelling.

Ages 11-16

Industrialisation, labour, global trade, flooding, pollution, recovery, oral history and evidence.

Community and adult groups

Memory mapping, belonging, urban change, creative ageing, resilience and environmental justice.

A reassuring welcome

Parents and adults must accompany and remain responsible for children. The route is short, step-free, pedestrian only and fully fenced. Toilets, refreshments and warm indoor space are available at Aviva Studios.
