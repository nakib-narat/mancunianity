Families, friends and curious minds

A Christmas experience for all generations

Come with children, parents, grandparents, a partner, friend or neighbour. Or book one place and come on your own. Listen, notice, compare and discover the River Irwell together.

Book ticketsView dates, prices and times

Meet at The Social, Aviva Studios. The address is The Social, Aviva Studios, Water Street, Manchester M3 4JQ.

Adult £15 · Senior aged 65 and over £12 · Child or young person aged 3–17 £5 · Under three: free, but a free ticket must be booked · Family Bundle £38 for two adults and two children or young people aged 3–17 · Access Companion: free, but a free ticket must be booked

Under-threes and Access Companions attend free, but a free ticket must still be booked for every person attending.

Anyone aged 17 or under must attend with a responsible adult aged 18 or over. Children and young people must remain with their accompanying adult throughout the experience. Accompanying adults are responsible for their supervision and safety.

Please arrive at The Social, Aviva Studios, from 10:00 am for the 10:30 am performance, or from 1:00 pm for the 1:30 pm performance.

The 30-minute arrival period allows time for check-in and wireless headset collection. We recommend checking in no later than 10:25 am for the morning performance or 1:25 pm for the afternoon performance.

The walking group leaves the meeting point when the performance starts at 10:30 am and 1:30 pm.

Different generations notice different things

A bridge may prompt one person to think about engineering, another to notice a train and someone else to remember how the riverside used to look. The experience leaves room for those different observations.
