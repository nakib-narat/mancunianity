Practical information for your group

Adult supervision
Anyone aged 17 or under must attend with a responsible adult aged 18 or over. Children and young people must remain with their accompanying adult throughout the experience. Accompanying adults are responsible for their supervision and safety.
Activities and headsets
Children receive a spotting activity. Wireless headsets are supplied and cleaned between uses; no app or personal audio equipment is needed.
Route and duration
The traffic-free pedestrian route is less than a quarter of a mile. The complete experience usually lasts about 65 minutes; allow up to 70 minutes, including the final 15-minute musical finale.
Outdoor conditions and seating
The experience continues in most weather. Bring warm, waterproof clothing and suitable footwear. Finale seating is on outdoor concrete seat blocks; a small waterproof cushion or folded sit mat may add comfort.
Late arrival
You may be able to catch up if you arrive after the performance starts, but joining the group and receiving a headset cannot be guaranteed. Follow directions from Mancunianity or Aviva Studios staff where available.

Pushchair suitability, child-sized headsets and family facilities have not been confirmed. Email contact@mancunianity.uk with questions before booking if these details affect your plans.

Explore the Irwell together

Different generations notice different things

A bridge may prompt one person to think about engineering, another to notice a train and someone else to remember how the riverside used to look. The experience leaves room for those different observations.

Who is it for?

Children and adults can enjoy the same story differently

The storytelling is designed to work particularly well for curious children aged 8 and over, teenagers and adults. This describes educational suitability, not ticket eligibility. No previous knowledge of Manchester, Salford, history, theatre or music is needed. You do not need to bring a child; adults attending with friends, relatives or on their own are equally welcome.

Good to know

  • Children and young people must attend with a responsible adult.
  • Solo bookings are welcome.
  • Singing is optional.
  • No phone or app is needed.
  • Wireless headsets are supplied.

Our river, our climate, our future

Notice the living river

The Irwell connects industrial history with present-day questions about flooding, wildlife, green space, walking and how two cities live with water.

Flood detective

Look for walls, changes in ground level and places where water might move when the river rises.

Nature connections

Notice plants, birds, river edges and the links between waterways and green spaces.

Future city

Imagine safer, greener routes connecting the Irwell with canals, parks, viaducts and neighbourhoods.

Schools and community groups

Use the river as a starting point

The growing learning collection can support local history, transport, environment, oral history, creativity and conversations about Manchester and Salford.

Learning groups aged 7 to 11

River observation, maps, wildlife, transport chains and local-history storytelling.

Learning groups aged 11 to 16

Industrialisation, labour, trade, flooding, pollution, recovery, oral history and evidence.

Community and adult groups

Memory mapping, belonging, urban change, creative ageing, resilience and environmental justice.
