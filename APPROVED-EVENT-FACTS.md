## Route, equipment and finale

- The pedestrian route is traffic-free and less than a quarter of a mile.
- Wireless headsets are supplied and cleaned between uses.
- No app needs to be downloaded; visitors do not need personal audio equipment.
- Children receive a spotting activity.
- The experience concludes with a 15-minute interactive riverside musical
  finale.
- Finale seating is on outdoor concrete seat blocks. Visitors may wish to bring
  a small waterproof cushion or folded sit mat for additional comfort.

Do not infer universal accessibility, absence of bicycles or public
pedestrians, absence of service access, or that children may walk without adult
supervision. Preserve accurate existing access information.

## Artistic description

Preserve this website copy, including paragraph order and punctuation:

> Mancunianity Flows is an immersive audio-theatre journey through one
> extraordinary quarter-mile of the River Irwell, where nearly 200 years of
> transport, industry, architecture, ecology and Manchester-Salford connections
> sit almost on top of one another.
>
> Wear a supplied wireless headset and follow the Irwell through storytelling,
> local voices, songs, music and a specially created atmospheric soundscape.
> The landscape is not scenery. The real river, bridges, railway arches,
> waterways and buildings around you are the stage.
>
> No phone, app or personal headphones are needed. Wireless headphones are
> supplied and hygienically prepared before each experience.
