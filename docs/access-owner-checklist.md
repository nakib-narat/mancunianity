# Access information: owner confirmation checklist

Internal working checklist for access facts that are not yet owner-confirmed.
Do not present pending items as event facilities, policies or route conditions.
Update `access.html` only when the owner confirms a fact.

## Meeting point and arrival

- [ ] Confirm where visitors should wait when they first arrive (inside or
  outside) and any venue-approved entrance wording.
- [ ] Confirm what visitors should look for to find the group.
- [ ] Confirm staff position and a day-of-event contact process if someone
  cannot find the group.
- [ ] Confirm accessible drop-off arrangements.
- [ ] Confirm public-transport directions.
- [ ] Confirm nearest accessible parking.

The owner supplied and identified
`images/access/aviva-studios-riverside-entrance.jpeg` as the Riverside entrance
meeting point. The meeting point and photograph are included in the public
guide; this does not establish a map pin or arrival procedure.

## Route and mobility

- [ ] Confirm route distance; do not substitute a previously published estimate
  unless the owner verifies it.
- [ ] Confirm surface material and condition.
- [ ] Confirm narrowest route width, gradients and camber.
- [ ] Confirm riverside barrier details and road crossings.
- [ ] Confirm pushchair suitability.
- [ ] Confirm manual/powered wheelchair and mobility-scooter suitability.
- [ ] Confirm route passing, turning, rest and early-exit arrangements.

## Facilities and support

- [ ] Confirm nearest accessible toilet and any Changing Places facility.
- [ ] Confirm seating type, location, availability, transfer arrangements and
  rest opportunities.
- [ ] Confirm assistance-dog policy.
- [ ] Confirm quiet waiting or sensory-break arrangements.
- [ ] Confirm early-exit and emergency procedures.

## Audio, hearing and sensory information

- [ ] Confirm headphone volume adjustment and fit.
- [ ] Confirm hearing-aid and neck-loop compatibility.
- [ ] Confirm whether a transcript is available during the experience.
- [ ] Confirm BSL or other alternative interpretation.
- [ ] Confirm sound levels, sudden sounds and other relevant sensory content.
- [ ] If no equivalent alternative to the headphone audio is offered, confirm
  the wording and add an alternative-format improvement to the project backlog.

## Review

- [ ] Recheck public access wording against confirmed information before
  publication.
- [ ] Update the "Last reviewed" date in `access.html` when confirmed facts
  change.
- [ ] Record the confirmation source/date in the event facts before treating a
  pending item as approved.
