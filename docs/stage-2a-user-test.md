# Stage 2A visitor user-test plan

No user testing has taken place. Use these tasks to evaluate whether a visitor
can complete the core Discover → Decide → Book → Prepare → Arrive journey.

Recruit five to eight participants, including where possible a parent or
carer, a visitor aged 65 or over, a disabled visitor, a first-time visitor
unfamiliar with Mancunianity, and a teacher or home educator. Ask participants
to think aloud, record observed actions and misunderstandings, and do not
coach them toward the answer.

## Tasks

### 1. Explain what Mancunianity Flows is

- **Success condition:** Participant describes an outdoor River Irwell
  audio-theatre experience with a riverside walk and live musical finale.
- **Required information:** Experience format, location context, audio and
  finale.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Mistaking it for a self-guided walk, indoor
  performance or learning platform.
- **Observation notes:** Record where the participant first finds the
  description and any terms they cannot explain.
- **Follow-up question:** What would you expect to see or do during the
  experience?

### 2. Find the morning performance start

- **Success condition:** Participant identifies 10:30 am as the start, not
  the arrival time.
- **Required information:** Morning performance start.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Treating 10:00 am as the performance start.
- **Observation notes:** Record which label or page resolves the distinction.
- **Follow-up question:** What made the start time clear or unclear?

### 3. Find when morning check-in opens

- **Success condition:** Participant identifies 10:00 am as morning arrival
  and check-in opening.
- **Required information:** Arrival/check-in versus start distinction.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Treating the recommended latest check-in as a
  door closing time.
- **Observation notes:** Record whether the participant notices the 10:25 am
  recommendation and how they interpret it.
- **Follow-up question:** How would you describe when you should arrive?

### 4. Find the meeting point

- **Success condition:** Participant gives The Social, Aviva Studios, Water
  Street, Manchester M3 4JQ.
- **Required information:** Venue name and full address.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Confusing the wider venue name with the meeting
  point.
- **Observation notes:** Record whether the address is easy to scan and
  accurately repeat.
- **Follow-up question:** Is there anything else you would need to know to
  find the meeting point?

### 5. Find the correct ticket for a three-year-old

- **Success condition:** Participant selects the child or young person ticket
  aged 3–17 at £5.
- **Required information:** Age band and price.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Thinking the Family Bundle is the only family
  option or that age three qualifies for the free under-three ticket.
- **Observation notes:** Record the pages and labels used to decide.
- **Follow-up question:** Was it clear which ticket applies to a child aged
  three?

### 6. Determine whether an under-three needs a ticket

- **Success condition:** Participant says the ticket is free but still must
  be booked.
- **Required information:** Under-three rule.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Assuming free means no booking is needed.
- **Observation notes:** Record whether the requirement appears near the price
  and booking action.
- **Follow-up question:** What would you do when booking for an under-three?

### 7. Find what happens in severe weather

- **Success condition:** Participant finds that if severe weather makes a
  performance unsafe, affected ticket holders will be contacted by email and
  offered a transfer subject to availability or a refund.
- **Required information:** Weather and cancellation summary.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Assuming the event always proceeds or that a
  transfer is guaranteed.
- **Observation notes:** Record how easily the participant finds and
  accurately summarises the policy.
- **Follow-up question:** Is the weather information enough to help you
  prepare?

### 8. Start the official booking journey

- **Success condition:** Participant activates the direct TryBooking event
  listing and understands it opens in a new tab.
- **Required information:** Direct event link, platform name and new-tab
  behaviour.
- **Likely misunderstanding:** Believing the link opens a TryBooking homepage
  or that the booking is completed on Mancunianity's site.
- **Observation notes:** Record the action label, link destination and
  understanding of the handoff.
- **Follow-up question:** What would you expect to happen after selecting a
  performance?

## Family-review checklist

- [ ] Ease of selecting tickets
- [ ] Clarity of age categories
- [ ] Adult supervision
- [ ] Route and duration
- [ ] Headsets and sound
- [ ] Concrete seating
- [ ] Weather
- [ ] Late arrival
- [ ] Child engagement
- [ ] Ease of finding access information

Do not describe the event as fully family friendly or best in class for
families unless actual family testing supports that claim.
