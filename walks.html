What happens?

Listen, discover, cross the river, then join the finale

About 50 minutes: follow the river

Starting from Aviva Studios, follow an easy-paced route of approximately a quarter mile. Through your own supplied wireless headset, hear commentary, local voices, songs and an atmospheric soundscape while the bridges, railways, waterways and buildings around you reveal their stories.

The journey moves from Manchester across the River Irwell into Salford.

No phone, app or personal headphones are needed. Wireless headphones are provided and hygienically prepared before each experience.

About 15 minutes: face the river

The walking stops and everyone takes a seat on the Salford side, facing the Irwell and Aviva Studios.

Here the new Mancunianity Flows song is unveiled. Join the chorus alongside the performers and Weavers, hum along quietly, or simply stand back, listen and soak in the music beside the water.

Experience the finale your way. Singing is optional, and listening is just as welcome.

Who is it for?

Mancunianity Flows is a festive experience for all generations. The storytelling is designed to entertain curious older children, teenagers and adults while giving families plenty to notice and talk about together.

  • Come with children, parents or grandparents
  • Come with a partner, friend or neighbour
  • Come on your own. Solo bookings are very welcome
  • No previous knowledge of Manchester, Salford, history, theatre or music is needed
  • Children must be accompanied by a responsible adult

We currently expect the storytelling to work particularly well for ages 8+ through adults, while younger family members are also welcome with their adults.

Manchester-weather ready

Meet at the riverside entrance of Aviva Studios 20 minutes before setting off. Your hosts will welcome you and help you gather inside the warm, step-free Social before the walk. Then take a gentle, easy-paced 400-metre riverside stroll and return to Aviva Studios after the finale for indoor warmth and refreshments.

Wear warm layers, waterproof clothing and comfortable footwear for Manchester in December. Ordinary rain will not stop the experience.

Severe weather or unsafe river conditions may require a change, postponement or cancellation. If that happens, booked visitors will be contacted using the details supplied at booking.

If you prefer extra comfort for the seated finale, bring a small waterproof sit-mat or cushion.

Start, finish and timing

Meet at the riverside entrance of Aviva Studios, Water Street, Manchester M3 4JG.

Please arrive 20 minutes before your booked departure. The experience itself is approximately 65 minutes: around 50 minutes moving through the landscape followed by a 15-minute seated musical finale.

Allow around 90 minutes in total for arrival, the experience and departure.

After the finale, return to Aviva Studios for toilets, refreshments and a warm, dry space.
