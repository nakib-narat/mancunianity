What happens?

Listen, discover, cross the river, then join the finale

The walking experience

Starting from Aviva Studios, follow an easy-paced, traffic-free pedestrian route of less than a quarter of a mile. Through a supplied wireless headset, hear commentary, local voices, songs and an atmospheric soundscape while the bridges, railways, waterways and buildings around you reveal their stories.

The journey moves from Manchester across the River Irwell into Salford.

No phone, app or personal headphones are needed. Wireless headsets are supplied and cleaned between uses.

Children receive a spotting activity during the experience.

The musical finale

The walking stops on the Salford side, beside the River Irwell. The final 15 minutes are an interactive riverside musical finale, with seating on outdoor concrete seat blocks facing the river and Aviva Studios. Visitors may wish to bring a small waterproof cushion or folded sit mat for additional comfort.

Here the new Mancunianity Flows song is unveiled. Join the chorus, hum along quietly, or simply stand back, listen and soak in the music beside the water. Singing is optional, and listening is just as welcome.

Who is it for?

Mancunianity Flows is a festive experience for all generations. The storytelling is designed to entertain curious older children, teenagers and adults while giving families plenty to notice and talk about together.

  • Come with children, parents or grandparents
  • Come with a partner, friend or neighbour
  • Come on your own. Solo bookings are very welcome
  • No previous knowledge of Manchester, Salford, history, theatre or music is needed
  • Anyone aged 17 or under must attend with a responsible adult aged 18 or over

Children and young people must remain with their accompanying adult throughout the experience. Accompanying adults are responsible for their supervision and safety.

Manchester-weather ready

Meet at The Social, Aviva Studios. The Social is the meeting and check-in point for Mancunianity Flows.

The experience continues in most weather, so come prepared for the forecast with warm, waterproof clothing and suitable footwear.

If severe weather makes a performance unsafe, affected ticket holders will be contacted by email and offered a transfer to another performance, subject to availability, or a refund.

If you prefer extra comfort for the seated finale, bring a small waterproof sit-mat or cushion.
