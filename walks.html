Family ticket: two adults and two children or young people aged 3–17. Smaller and differently shaped family groups can simply combine £15 adult and £5 child tickets.

Important information

  • Family-pitched storytelling expected to entertain ages 8+ through adults
  • Children must be accompanied by a responsible adult
  • Capacity limited to 35 people per walk
  • Quarter-mile, step-free, easy-paced route
  • Pedestrian-only maintained paths, fully fenced
  • Audiences will not be required to stand during the concert; smooth stone seating is available, and wheelchair users may remain in their own chair
  • Bring a waterproof sit-mat or cushion if preferred
  • Chocolate Santa for every attending child; allergen details at booking

Manchester-weather ready

Ordinary rain will not stop the event. Wear warm layers, waterproof clothing and comfortable footwear. Severe weather or unsafe river conditions may require a change, postponement or cancellation.
