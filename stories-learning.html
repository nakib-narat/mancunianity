About the collection

Made to be useful beyond the live programme

Mancunianity Flows brings researched history into conversation with lived experience. The collection supports family discovery, school learning, community activity and reflection on the river's future.

Accurate

Established facts, interpretations and open questions are clearly distinguished.

Accessible

Material is written for different ages, devices and ways of learning.

Participatory

Local knowledge and the Weavers' observations help guide the work.

Ethical

Permission, privacy, safeguarding and respectful representation guide publication.

Reusable

Resources are easy to find, print and adapt where rights permit.

Maintained

Material is reviewed, corrected and developed as new evidence emerges.
