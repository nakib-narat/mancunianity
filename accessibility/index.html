Access to information and the website

Accessibility statement

Our approach to accessible information, known limitations and ways to request another format.

Prepared: 7 September 2026

We are working to make this website conform as closely as practicable to WCAG 2.2 Level AA. This is a target, not a claim that every applicable criterion has been independently audited.

What we have done

  • Semantic headings and landmarks
  • Keyboard-accessible links and navigation
  • Visible focus styles
  • High-contrast colours
  • Responsive reflow and text resizing
  • Skip-to-content link
  • Meaningful link text
  • No autoplay media
  • Alternative text for the illustrated map

The illustrated map

The map is a visual introduction rather than final turn-by-turn navigation. A fuller text alternative and detailed route information are in development and will require separate access, safety and accuracy checks before release.

Planning a future visit

The experience is being developed for an outdoor urban riverside setting. Final information about distance, duration, surfaces, seating, toilets, weather, rest opportunities and any alternative route will be published after final checks. Please do not rely on the illustrated map alone for independent navigation.

Known limitations

Downloadable documents, audio, video and the independent walk guide are not yet published. Each will require a separate accessibility and permissions review before release. The live site has not yet undergone an independent full WCAG audit.

Request another format or report a problem

Email nakib.narat@btinternet.com or call 07538 599049. Please describe the page, format or barrier and what would help.
