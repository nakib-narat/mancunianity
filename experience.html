The experience

A unique walking experience

An easy, step-free quarter-mile audio-theatre walk through extraordinary stories, followed by a seated 15-minute riverside concert. The guided walk and concert last approximately 60-75 minutes. Please arrive 20 minutes early, so allow about 90 minutes in total.

What happens?

Arrive at Aviva Studios

Arrive 20 minutes early for a warm welcome, check-in, headset fitting and route briefing. Walks leave promptly.

Step towards the River Irwell

Follow safe, maintained, pedestrian-only paths through a landscape of bridges, waterways and city views.

Listen through wireless headphones

Hear an original soundscape, music, field recordings, local voices and remarkable stories about people, buildings and animals.

Notice and discover together

Take part in gentle family prompts, conversation and observation. Participation is friendly and optional.

Finish with a riverside concert

Enjoy 15 minutes of live music on the steps overlooking the Irwell: a sea shanty, Manchester and Salford songs and a group singalong.

Return to Aviva Studios

Return for toilets, refreshments, a warm dry space and time to continue the conversation.
